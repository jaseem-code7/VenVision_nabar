# VenVision_nabar

Venvision Luxury Jewellery E-commerce Navigation & Mega Menu Demo.

## Highlights
- **Luxury Saudi Jewellery Aesthetic**: Warm beige/ivory foundation with signature burgundy (`#650006`) & gold accents.
- **Interactive Multi-Level Mega Menu**: Dynamic filtering across Category, Occasion, Price, Gender, Communities, and Metals.
- **Curated High-Definition Photography**: Close-up and lifestyle imagery for Rings, Earrings, Necklaces, Bracelets, Bangles, Pendants, Wedding Jewellery, Men's, Women's, Kids, and Natural Gemstones.
- **Bespoke Jewellery Customization CTA**: Integrated atelier CTA strip linked to `customization.html`.
- **Live Gold Benchmark & Responsive Layout**: Clean interaction design adhering to premium jewellery store standards.
